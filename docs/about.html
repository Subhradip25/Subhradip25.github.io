I believe the most urgent problems in agriculture - land degradation, climate vulnerability, soil health decline - are fundamentally spatial problems that demand open-source geospatial solutions. My work sits at the intersection of remote sensing, agronomy, and natural resource management, translating satellite data into actionable insights for food security and sustainable development. I am driven by the conviction that science done openly, reproducibly, and in service of SDG targets is science that truly matters.
